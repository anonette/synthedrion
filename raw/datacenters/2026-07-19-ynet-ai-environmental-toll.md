# AI's environmental toll: key findings from a UN report

> Source: https://www.ynet.co.il/digital/technews/article/b1008dswmgx
> Collected: 2026-07-19
> Topic Tags: infrastructure, energy, water, e-waste, geopolitics

## Summary

Reports on a UN study of AI infrastructure's environmental footprint by 2030:

- **Water**: AI infrastructure could consume water equivalent to the basic needs of 1.5 billion people by 2030 (cooling systems + power generation).
- **Energy**: AI data centers may consume ~945 terawatt-hours annually by 2030 — roughly triple Pakistan, Bangladesh, and Nigeria's combined electricity usage. (Matches the 945 TWh IEA figure already cited in this wiki's energy page.)
- **Land**: AI infrastructure could occupy over 14,500 km² by 2030 — about 12x New York City's area, two-thirds of Israel's total land area.
- **Usage pattern**: 80-90% of AI energy consumption comes from everyday user inference, not model training; one unnamed popular service processes ~2.5 billion requests/day.
- **E-waste**: AI systems may generate 2.5 million tons of e-waste annually by 2030, much destined for developing nations lacking disposal infrastructure.
- **Global inequality**: over 90% of AI computing power is concentrated in the US and China; 150+ countries lack significant AI infrastructure — wealthier nations capture AI's benefits while poorer regions bear extraction/waste costs.
