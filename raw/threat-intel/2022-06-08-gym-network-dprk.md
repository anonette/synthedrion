On 8 June 2022, the Gym Network was targeted in a sophisticated crypto hack, resulting in the theft of approximately $2.1 million USD. The attack was executed by exploiting vulnerabilities within the network's infrastructure, leading to significant financial losses for the platform and its users. The immediate impact included a halt in operations and a sharp decline in user trust and token value.

The attack leveraged a combination of smart contract vulnerabilities and potentially compromised private keys. The attacker executed a series of transactions that manipulated the network's protocols, allowing unauthorised access to funds. The exact exploit mechanism involved the use of reentrancy attacks and flash loans, which are common in such breaches.

Stolen funds were rapidly moved through a series of transactions involving multiple wallets and blockchain networks. The attacker utilised bridges and mixers to obscure the fund trail, eventually directing the assets to centralised exchanges for cash-out. Key infrastructure used included the Poly Network and various DeFi protocols.

The attack is suspected to be orchestrated by the hacker group APT38, known for similar exploits. This attribution is based on the use of specific TTPs and infrastructure overlaps with previous incidents attributed to this group. Confidence in this attribution is medium, pending further investigation.
