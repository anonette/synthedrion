On 8 January 2025, the Moby Trade platform experienced a significant crypto hack, resulting in the theft of substantial funds. The attack targeted the platform's infrastructure, exploiting vulnerabilities to siphon off assets. The immediate financial impact was severe, with over $1,003,080.00 in USD equivalent stolen.

The attack was executed through a series of sophisticated transactions that exploited weaknesses in the platform's security protocols. The exact mechanism involved the use of compromised private keys and potentially a smart contract vulnerability, although specific contract functions exploited were not detailed in the available data.

Stolen funds were rapidly moved through a series of transactions involving multiple wallets and intermediary addresses. The funds were laundered using a combination of bridge hopping and mixer services, eventually reaching centralised exchanges (CEXs) for cash-out. Notable infrastructure used includes the Poly Network and various high-volume intermediaries.

The attack is suspected to be linked to the APT38 group, known for its sophisticated cyber operations and previous involvement in similar incidents. The use of advanced laundering techniques and infrastructure overlaps with known APT38 operations supports this attribution.
