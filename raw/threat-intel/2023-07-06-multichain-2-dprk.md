On 6 July 2023, a significant exploit targeted the Multichain protocol, resulting in the theft of approximately $126.3 million USD. The attack was executed by exploiting a vulnerability within the protocol's infrastructure, leading to a substantial financial impact on the protocol and its users. The immediate financial loss was significant, affecting the protocol's operations and user trust.

The attack was executed through a series of transactions that exploited a weakness in the protocol's smart contract infrastructure. The specific vulnerability exploited remains unknown, but the attack involved rapid transaction sequences and the use of sophisticated blockchain manipulation techniques, potentially involving private key compromise or smart contract function abuse.

The stolen funds were initially moved from the exploit wallet to intermediary wallets, employing a series of layering techniques including bridge hopping and mixing. The funds were then distributed across multiple chains and eventually cashed out through various exchanges. Key infrastructure used included known bridges and mixers, indicating a premeditated laundering strategy.

The attack is attributed to the Lazarus Group, a well-known cybercrime organisation with a history of targeting blockchain platforms. This attribution is based on transaction patterns, infrastructure overlaps, and historical behaviour consistent with the group's known tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs).
