On 10 November 2023, Poloniex, a cryptocurrency exchange, experienced a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $126 million USD. The attack was attributed to the Lazarus Group, a well-known cybercrime syndicate. The exploit mechanism remains unspecified, but the immediate financial impact was substantial, affecting numerous users and stakeholders.

The technical execution of the attack is not fully detailed in the available data. However, the involvement of the Lazarus Group suggests the use of sophisticated techniques, potentially involving private key compromise or infrastructure vulnerabilities. The absence of specific smart contract or infrastructure failures in the data indicates a possible off-chain attack vector.

Stolen funds were rapidly moved from the exploit wallet through a series of intermediary wallets, employing layering techniques such as mixing and bridging across multiple blockchains. The funds were eventually directed towards exchanges and other cash-out points, indicating a well-planned laundering strategy.

The Lazarus Group is suspected with high confidence due to the use of known TTPs and infrastructure overlaps with previous incidents. Their historical involvement in similar high-profile cryptocurrency thefts supports this attribution.
