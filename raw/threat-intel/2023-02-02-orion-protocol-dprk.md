On 2 February 2023, Orion Protocol experienced a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $3,000,000.00 USD. The attack targeted the protocol's smart contract infrastructure, exploiting vulnerabilities to siphon funds into attacker-controlled wallets. The immediate financial impact was substantial, affecting both the protocol's operations and its user base.

The attack was executed through a sophisticated exploitation of smart contract vulnerabilities, potentially involving reentrancy attacks or access control failures. The exact mechanism remains under investigation, but initial analysis suggests the use of automated scripts to execute rapid transactions, bypassing standard security checks.

Stolen funds were quickly moved through a series of transactions involving multiple wallets and blockchain networks. The attacker utilised known laundering techniques, including bridge hopping and mixer usage, to obscure the fund trail. Key infrastructure such as the Ethereum network and various DeFi protocols were employed to facilitate the laundering process.

The Lazarus Group, a well-known cybercrime syndicate, is suspected of orchestrating the attack. This attribution is based on the use of similar tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) observed in previous incidents attributed to the group. The confidence level in this attribution is medium, pending further evidence.
