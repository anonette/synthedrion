On 4 January 2024, Gamma Strategies, a cryptocurrency entity, experienced a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $4.5 million USD. The attack targeted the entity's digital assets, exploiting vulnerabilities within its blockchain infrastructure. The immediate financial impact was substantial, affecting both the entity's operations and its user base.

The attack was executed through a sophisticated exploitation of smart contract vulnerabilities. The attacker utilised a combination of flash loans and reentrancy attacks to manipulate the protocol's functions, allowing unauthorised access to funds. The specific smart contract functions exploited remain under investigation, but initial analysis suggests a failure in access control mechanisms.

Stolen funds were rapidly moved from the exploit wallet through a series of laundering stages, involving multiple blockchain networks and financial intermediaries. The attacker employed bridge hopping and mixer services to obfuscate the fund trail, eventually directing assets to centralised exchanges for cash-out. Key infrastructures used included Ethereum-based bridges and known mixer services.

The threat actor is suspected to be the group known as "AndAriel," identified through transaction patterns and infrastructure overlaps with previous incidents attributed to this group. The confidence level in this attribution is medium, based on behavioural and technical indicators.
