# Ronin Network (Axie Infinity bridge) — DPRK-attributed incident

> Type: threat-intel incident (SOURCED CLAIM — treat attribution as a claim at the stated confidence, not fact)
> Incident ID: CHA-Ronin-Network-149
> Timestamp: 2022-03-23T00:00:00Z
> Attribution: DPRK / Lazarus Group (confidence: 0.8)
> Target: Ronin Network (Axie Infinity bridge)
> Amount (USD): $625,000,000
> Asset: Ethereum
> Vector: bridge exploit
> References: Ronin Network – Blockchain Forensics Intelligence Report (https://sandbox.hacksleuths.com/LLM_REPORTS/Ronin_Network/Ronin_Network_report.html)

On 23 March 2022, the Ronin Network, a blockchain protocol known for its bridge services, suffered a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $625 million USD in cryptocurrency assets. The attack targeted the bridge infrastructure, exploiting vulnerabilities to siphon funds from the network.

The attack was executed by exploiting a vulnerability in the Ronin Network's bridge contract, allowing the attacker to bypass security protocols and authorise fraudulent transactions. The exploit involved the compromise of private keys associated with validator nodes, enabling the attacker to forge withdrawals.

Stolen funds were initially moved through a series of rapid transactions across multiple wallets, employing techniques such as bridge hopping and mixer usage to obfuscate the trail. The funds were subsequently laundered through various decentralised exchanges (DEXs) and centralised exchanges (CEXs), with a significant portion routed through known laundering infrastructure.

The attack is attributed to the Lazarus Group, a North Korean state-sponsored hacking group, based on the use of similar tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) observed in previous incidents. The group's involvement is further supported by infrastructure overlaps and historical patterns of targeting cryptocurrency platforms.
