On 7 August 2023, the Steadefi protocol experienced a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $1,140,000.00 USD. The attack was executed by the hacker group APT38, known for its sophisticated cyber operations. The immediate financial impact was substantial, affecting the protocol's liquidity and user trust.

The attack exploited a vulnerability within the Steadefi protocol, although the specific exploit mechanism remains unknown. The transactions involved suggest a potential manipulation of smart contract functions or a private key compromise, given the rapid and coordinated movement of funds.

Stolen funds were initially moved from the exploit wallet to intermediary wallets, employing a series of layering techniques including bridge hopping and mixer usage. The funds traversed multiple chains and utilised various decentralised exchanges (DEXs) to obfuscate the trail before reaching centralised exchanges (CEXs) for cash-out.

APT38 is suspected with high confidence due to the use of known TTPs associated with this group, including sophisticated laundering techniques and infrastructure overlaps with previous incidents. The group's historical focus on financial gain through cyber means supports this attribution.
