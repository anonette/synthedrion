On 5 May 2023, the Deus DAO protocol was compromised in a sophisticated cyber attack resulting in the theft of approximately $6,500,000.00. The attack was executed by exploiting a vulnerability within the protocol's smart contract infrastructure, leading to unauthorised fund transfers to wallets controlled by the threat actor.

The attack leveraged a smart contract vulnerability, potentially involving reentrancy or access control failures. The attacker utilised a series of rapid transactions to drain funds from the protocol, exploiting weaknesses in the contract's logic to bypass security checks and authorise illegitimate transfers.

Stolen funds were initially moved from the exploit wallet to intermediary addresses, employing a series of transactions designed to obfuscate the trail. The funds were subsequently laundered through various blockchain networks, utilising bridges and mixers to complicate tracing efforts. Key infrastructure used included the Poly Network and other high-volume intermediaries.

The attack is attributed to the hacker group APT38 with a high confidence level. This attribution is supported by the use of known APT38 tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs), including the specific laundering methods and infrastructure overlaps with previous incidents linked to the group.
