The Sovryn incident involved a sophisticated crypto hack targeting the Sovryn protocol, a decentralised finance (DeFi) platform operating on the Bitcoin smart contract network. The attack occurred on 4 October 2022, resulting in the theft of significant funds. The immediate financial impact was substantial, with the stolen assets valued at approximately $1,111,000.00 USD.

The attack exploited vulnerabilities within the Sovryn protocol, potentially involving smart contract manipulation or private key compromise. The exact exploit mechanism remains under investigation, but the rapid sequence of transactions suggests a premeditated attack leveraging automated scripts.

Stolen funds were swiftly moved through a series of transactions involving multiple wallets and blockchain networks. The attacker utilised known laundering techniques such as bridge hopping and mixer usage to obfuscate the fund trail. Key infrastructure included the use of the Ethereum network and various DeFi protocols for layering and integration.

The threat actor is suspected to be the Kimsuky group, known for its sophisticated cyber capabilities and previous involvement in similar incidents. The attribution is based on transaction patterns and infrastructure overlaps with prior attacks attributed to this group.
