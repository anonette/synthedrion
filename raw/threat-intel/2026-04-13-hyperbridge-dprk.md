# Hyperbridge — DPRK-attributed incident

> Type: threat-intel incident (SOURCED CLAIM — treat attribution as a claim at the stated confidence, not fact)
> Incident ID: CHA-Hyperbridge-82
> Timestamp: 2026-04-13T00:00:00Z
> Attribution: DPRK / Lazarus Group (confidence: 0.8)
> Target: Hyperbridge
> Amount (USD): $2,500,000
> Asset: Ethereum
> Vector: bridge exploit
> References: Hyperbridge – Blockchain Forensics Intelligence Report (https://sandbox.hacksleuths.com/LLM_REPORTS/Hyperbridge/Hyperbridge_report.html)

On 13 April 2026, the Hyperbridge protocol suffered a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $2.5 million USD in various cryptocurrencies. The attack targeted the protocol's bridge infrastructure, exploiting vulnerabilities to siphon funds from user accounts. The immediate financial impact was severe, affecting numerous users and causing a temporary halt in operations.

The attack was executed by exploiting a vulnerability in the bridge's smart contract, potentially involving a reentrancy attack or a flaw in access control mechanisms. The attacker utilised sophisticated scripts to automate the exploit, allowing for rapid execution and fund extraction.

Stolen funds were initially moved through a series of rapid transactions across multiple wallets, employing techniques such as bridge hopping and mixer usage to obfuscate the trail. The funds were eventually routed through various decentralised exchanges (DEXs) and centralised exchanges (CEXs) for laundering and cash-out.

The attack is suspected to be orchestrated by the Lazarus Group, a well-known cybercrime syndicate with a history of targeting blockchain infrastructures. The use of advanced laundering techniques and infrastructure overlaps with previous incidents attributed to this group support this hypothesis.
