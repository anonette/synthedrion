# Eleven Finance — DPRK-attributed incident

> Type: threat-intel incident (SOURCED CLAIM — treat attribution as a claim at the stated confidence, not fact)
> Incident ID: CHA-Eleven-Finance-59
> Timestamp: 2021-06-22T00:00:00Z
> Attribution: DPRK / APT38 (confidence: 0.4)
> Target: Eleven Finance
> Amount (USD): $4,500,000
> Asset: Ethereum
> Vector: unknown
> References: Eleven Finance – Blockchain Forensics Intelligence Report (https://sandbox.hacksleuths.com/LLM_REPORTS/Eleven_Finance/Eleven_Finance_report.html)

On 22 June 2021, Eleven Finance, a decentralised finance (DeFi) protocol, experienced a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $4.5 million USD. The attack targeted the protocol's smart contracts, exploiting vulnerabilities to siphon funds. The immediate financial impact was substantial, affecting both the protocol's liquidity and its user base.

The attack was executed by exploiting a vulnerability in Eleven Finance's smart contracts. The specific weakness involved was likely related to improper access controls or a reentrancy flaw, allowing the attacker to manipulate contract functions to drain funds. The attack was sophisticated, involving multiple transactions executed in rapid succession.

Stolen funds were initially moved through a series of rapid transactions across multiple wallets. The attacker utilised various blockchain bridges and mixers to obfuscate the trail, eventually attempting to cash out through centralised exchanges. Key infrastructure used included the Binance Smart Chain and Ethereum network bridges.

The attack is suspected to have been carried out by the hacker group APT38, known for targeting financial institutions. This attribution is based on the use of similar tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) observed in previous incidents attributed to this group. The confidence level in this attribution is medium.
