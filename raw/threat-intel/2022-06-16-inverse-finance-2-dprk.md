On 16 June 2022, Inverse Finance, a decentralised finance (DeFi) protocol, experienced a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $5.8 million USD. The attack was executed by exploiting a vulnerability in the protocol's smart contracts, leading to unauthorised fund transfers.

The attack involved a sophisticated exploitation of the protocol's smart contracts, potentially utilising flash loans and price manipulation techniques. The specific vulnerability exploited remains unclear due to the absence of detailed technical documentation in the source files.

Stolen funds were rapidly moved through multiple blockchain addresses, employing a series of layering techniques including token swaps and transfers across different chains. The funds were routed through critical bridges and mixers, complicating traceability.

The attack is attributed to the hacker group APT38, known for its sophisticated cyber operations and previous involvement in similar incidents. The attribution is supported by transaction patterns and infrastructure overlaps with past attacks linked to this group.
