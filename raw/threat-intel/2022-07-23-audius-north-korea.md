# Audius — North Korea-attributed incident

> Timestamp: 2022-07-23T00:00:00Z
> Attribution: North Korea / unknown group (confidence: unstated)

> Asset: Ethereum
> Vector: unknown
> References: Audius – Blockchain Forensics Intelligence Report (https://sandbox.hacksleuths.com/LLM_REPORTS/Audius/Audius_report.html)

On 23 July 2022, the Audius protocol experienced a significant security breach resulting in the unauthorised transfer of approximately $6,000,000.00 in digital assets. The attack targeted the protocol's infrastructure, exploiting vulnerabilities to siphon funds into attacker-controlled wallets. The immediate financial impact was substantial, affecting both the protocol's operations and its user base.

The attack was executed through a series of rapid transactions exploiting a vulnerability in the Audius protocol's smart contract infrastructure. The specific exploit mechanism remains under investigation, but initial analysis suggests a potential access control failure or private key compromise. The attack involved multiple transactions executed in quick succession, indicating a premeditated and technically sophisticated approach.

Stolen funds were initially moved through a series of intermediary wallets, employing techniques such as bridge hopping and mixer usage to obfuscate the trail. The funds traversed multiple blockchain networks and utilised various decentralised exchanges (DEXs) and centralised exchanges (CEXs) for laundering. Key infrastructure used included Tornado Cash and other known mixing services.

The threat actor remains unidentified, but the use of sophisticated laundering techniques suggests involvement of a well-resourced and experienced group. The attack shares characteristics with previous incidents linked to known cybercriminal organisations, though no direct attribution has been made.
