# Mixin Network — North Korea-attributed incident

> Timestamp: 2023-09-23T00:00:00Z
> Attribution: North Korea / unknown group (confidence: unstated)
> Target: Mixin Network
> Amount (USD): $200,000,000
> Asset: Ethereum
> Vector: unknown
> References: Mixin Network – Blockchain Forensics Intelligence Report (https://sandbox.hacksleuths.com/LLM_REPORTS/Mixin_Network/Mixin_Network_report.html)

On 21 February 2026, the Mixin Network suffered a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $200 million in cryptocurrency assets. The attack targeted the network's infrastructure, exploiting vulnerabilities that allowed the attacker to siphon funds from user accounts. The immediate financial impact was severe, affecting both the network's liquidity and user trust.

The attack was executed through a sophisticated exploit that targeted a vulnerability within the Mixin Network's smart contract infrastructure. The attacker likely used a combination of flash loans and reentrancy attacks to manipulate the contract's state and extract funds. The precise technical details of the vulnerability remain under investigation, but initial analysis suggests a failure in access control mechanisms.

Stolen funds were rapidly moved through a series of transactions involving multiple blockchain networks and laundering techniques. The attacker utilised bridge hopping and mixer services to obfuscate the fund trail, eventually directing assets to centralised exchanges for cash-out.

While the identity of the attacker remains unknown, the tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) employed suggest a highly skilled individual or group with prior experience in blockchain exploits. The use of advanced laundering techniques and infrastructure overlaps with previous incidents indicates potential links to known cybercriminal groups.
