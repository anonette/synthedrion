# BitMart — DPRK-attributed incident

> Incident ID: CHA-BitMart-26
> Timestamp: 2021-12-04T00:00:00Z
> Attribution: DPRK / APT38 (confidence: 0.24)
> Target: BitMart
> Amount (USD): $196,000,000
> Asset: Ethereum
> Vector: unknown
> References: BitMart – Blockchain Forensics Intelligence Report (https://sandbox.hacksleuths.com/LLM_REPORTS/BitMart/BitMart_report.html)

On 4 December 2021, BitMart, a cryptocurrency exchange, experienced a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $196 million in digital assets. The attack targeted the exchange's hot wallets, exploiting vulnerabilities in the security infrastructure. The immediate financial impact was severe, affecting both the exchange's operations and its users' holdings.

The attack was executed through a sophisticated exploit involving the compromise of private keys associated with BitMart's hot wallets. The attackers utilised a combination of social engineering and technical vulnerabilities to gain access. The exact exploit mechanism remains under investigation, but it is suspected that a combination of phishing and infrastructure weaknesses were exploited.

Stolen funds were rapidly moved from the exploit wallet through a series of transactions involving multiple intermediary wallets. The attackers employed layering techniques, including the use of mixers and cross-chain bridges, to obfuscate the fund flow. The funds were eventually moved to various exchanges and off-ramp services for cash-out.

The attack is attributed to the hacker group APT38, known for its sophisticated cyber operations and links to state-sponsored activities. The attribution is based on the use of similar tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) observed in previous incidents linked to the group.
