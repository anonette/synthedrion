On 24 September 2025, SBI Crypto, a significant player in the cryptocurrency mining and trading sector, experienced a substantial security breach. The incident involved the unauthorised transfer of approximately $24 million in digital assets. The attack was executed by exploiting vulnerabilities in the company's blockchain infrastructure, leading to a swift and large-scale theft of funds. The immediate financial impact was severe, affecting both the company's operations and its stakeholders.

The attack was executed through a sophisticated exploitation of blockchain vulnerabilities, potentially involving private key compromise or smart contract manipulation. The exact exploit mechanism remains under investigation, but initial analysis suggests the use of automated scripts to facilitate rapid fund transfers across multiple wallets.

Stolen funds were quickly moved through a series of transactions involving multiple wallets and blockchain networks. The attackers utilised various laundering techniques, including bridge hopping and mixer services, to obfuscate the fund trail. Key infrastructure used included known cryptocurrency exchanges and decentralised finance (DeFi) protocols.

The attack is suspected to be linked to the Kimsuky group, a known cybercriminal organisation with a history of targeting financial institutions. The attribution is based on the use of similar tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) observed in previous incidents attributed to this group.
