On 20 May 2024, Gala Games, a blockchain-based gaming platform, experienced a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $216 million USD. The attack targeted the platform's smart contract infrastructure, exploiting a vulnerability that allowed unauthorised access to user funds. The immediate financial impact was severe, affecting both the platform's operations and its user base.

The attack was executed through a sophisticated exploit of the smart contract's access control mechanisms. The attacker utilised a combination of private key compromise and reentrancy attacks to drain funds from the platform. The exploit involved multiple transactions executed in rapid succession, indicating a high level of premeditation and technical expertise.

Stolen funds were initially moved from the exploit wallet to a series of intermediary wallets, employing complex layering techniques including bridge hopping and mixer usage. The funds traversed multiple blockchain networks and were eventually cashed out through centralised exchanges. The laundering process involved the use of known mixing services and cross-chain bridges to obfuscate the fund trail.

The attack is attributed to the Kimsuky group, a known cybercriminal organisation with a history of targeting blockchain platforms. The attribution is supported by the use of specific TTPs, including infrastructure overlaps and similar laundering patterns observed in previous incidents. The confidence level in this attribution is high.
