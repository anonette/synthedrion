On 7 November 2023, a significant crypto hack incident involving the entity known as RIP MEV Bot 2 was identified. The attack resulted in the theft of substantial funds, with the immediate financial impact estimated at $2,000,000.00 USD. The exploit targeted a decentralised finance (DeFi) protocol, leveraging vulnerabilities within the system to siphon funds.

The attack was executed through a series of transactions that exploited weaknesses in the protocol's smart contract infrastructure. The specific vulnerability exploited remains under investigation, but the attack involved rapid transaction sequences and potential use of automated scripts to manipulate the protocol's operations. No specific smart contract functions or private key compromises have been confirmed at this stage.

Stolen funds were initially moved from the exploit wallet to intermediary wallets, employing a strategy of rapid multi-hop transactions. The funds were subsequently layered through various mixers and bridges, including Tornado Cash, to obfuscate their origin. The final destinations of these funds remain under investigation, with some suspected to have reached centralised exchanges for cash-out.

The threat actor is suspected to be associated with the group APT38, known for its sophisticated cyber operations and previous involvement in similar incidents. The use of advanced laundering techniques and infrastructure overlaps with known APT38 operations support this attribution.
