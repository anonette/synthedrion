On 24 July 2025, the Woo X cryptocurrency exchange experienced a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $14,000,000.00. The attack was attributed to the Lazarus Group, a well-known cybercriminal organisation. The exploit mechanism involved a sophisticated series of transactions that rapidly moved funds through multiple wallets and exchanges.

The attack exploited vulnerabilities in the exchange's infrastructure, potentially involving compromised private keys or access control failures. The transactions were executed with high precision, indicating premeditated planning and advanced technical capabilities. No specific smart contract vulnerabilities were identified, suggesting the attack may have leveraged infrastructure weaknesses.

Stolen funds were initially moved from the exploit wallet to intermediary wallets, employing a series of rapid transactions to obfuscate the trail. The funds were then layered through various mixers and bridges, including potential use of offshore entities as identified in the Offshore Leaks databases. The final destinations included several centralised exchanges, where the funds were likely cashed out.

The Lazarus Group is suspected with high confidence due to the use of known TTPs and infrastructure overlaps with previous incidents attributed to this group. The group's history of targeting financial institutions and cryptocurrency exchanges supports this attribution.
