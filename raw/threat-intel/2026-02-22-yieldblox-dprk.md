On 22 February 2026, the Yieldblox protocol suffered a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $10,970,000.00 USD. The attack targeted the protocol's smart contract infrastructure, exploiting vulnerabilities to siphon funds into attacker-controlled wallets.

The attack was executed through a sophisticated exploitation of smart contract vulnerabilities, potentially involving reentrancy attacks or flash loan manipulations. The exact mechanism remains under investigation, but initial analysis suggests a failure in access control or improper handling of contract functions.

Stolen funds were rapidly moved through a series of transactions across multiple wallets, employing techniques such as bridge hopping and mixer usage to obscure the trail. The funds were eventually routed through various blockchain networks and potentially laundered through centralised exchanges.

The attack is suspected to be orchestrated by the hacker group "AndAriel", known for similar exploits in the past. The use of advanced laundering techniques and infrastructure overlaps with previous incidents supports this attribution.
