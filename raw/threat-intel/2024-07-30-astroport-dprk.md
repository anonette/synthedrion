The Astroport incident involved a significant crypto hack targeting the Astroport protocol, a decentralised exchange (DEX) operating on the Terra blockchain. The attack occurred on 30 July 2024, resulting in the theft of approximately $6.4 million USD. The exploit mechanism remains unclear, but the attack was attributed to the hacker group APT38, known for sophisticated cyber operations.

The attack exploited vulnerabilities within the Astroport protocol, potentially involving smart contract manipulation or private key compromise. The exact technical details of the exploit are not fully documented, but the rapid execution suggests premeditated planning and advanced technical capabilities.

Stolen funds were initially moved from the exploit wallet to a series of intermediary wallets, employing complex layering techniques across multiple blockchains. The funds were laundered through various bridges and mixers, eventually reaching centralised exchanges (CEXs) for cash-out. The use of multiple chains and sophisticated laundering methods indicates a high level of operational security.

APT38 is suspected of orchestrating the attack, with a high confidence level based on the use of known TTPs and infrastructure overlaps with previous incidents attributed to this group. The group's history of targeting financial institutions and their sophisticated laundering techniques support this attribution.
