On 21 February 2025, ByBit, a prominent cryptocurrency exchange, experienced a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $1.4 billion USD in digital assets. The attack was executed by the hacker group APT38, known for its sophisticated cyber operations. The exploit mechanism remains unknown, but the immediate financial impact was substantial, affecting both the exchange and its users.

The attack exploited a vulnerability within ByBit's infrastructure, potentially involving compromised private keys or a smart contract flaw. The technical specifics of the vulnerability remain undisclosed, but the rapid execution suggests premeditated planning and advanced technical capabilities.

Stolen funds were swiftly moved from the exploit wallet through a series of intermediary wallets, employing techniques such as bridge hopping and mixer usage to obfuscate the trail. The funds traversed multiple blockchains and utilised decentralised exchanges (DEXs) for laundering before reaching centralised exchanges (CEXs) for cash-out.

APT38, a group with a history of targeting financial institutions, is suspected with high confidence. This attribution is supported by the group's known tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs), including infrastructure overlaps and similar past incidents.
