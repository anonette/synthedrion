On 10 September 2024, a significant crypto hack incident targeted Indodax, a centralised cryptocurrency exchange. The attack resulted in the theft of approximately $25,220,000.00, impacting the exchange's operations and its users. The exploit mechanism involved a series of rapid transactions, indicating a well-coordinated attack.

The attack exploited vulnerabilities in the exchange's transaction processing system. The attackers utilised a series of normal transactions to siphon funds from the exchange. The specific technical weaknesses exploited remain under investigation, but the pattern suggests potential infrastructure failures or compromised private keys.

Stolen funds were moved through a series of transactions involving multiple wallets. The attackers employed layering techniques, including the use of mixers and bridges, to obfuscate the fund flow. The funds were eventually directed to various offshore entities, as identified in leak databases.

The attack is suspected to be orchestrated by the hacker group APT38, known for targeting financial institutions. The use of sophisticated laundering techniques and infrastructure overlaps with previous incidents attributed to this group supports this hypothesis.
