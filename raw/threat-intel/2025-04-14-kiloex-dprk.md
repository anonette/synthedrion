On 14 April 2025, KiloEx, a cryptocurrency exchange, experienced a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $7,491,500.00. The attack was executed by exploiting a vulnerability in the exchange's infrastructure, leading to unauthorised access and subsequent fund extraction.

The attack leveraged a sophisticated exploit involving the compromise of private keys, allowing the attacker to initiate transactions from the exchange's wallets. The exact mechanism involved manipulating transaction signatures to bypass security protocols.

Stolen funds were initially moved through a series of rapid transactions across multiple wallets, employing techniques such as bridge hopping and mixer usage to obfuscate the trail. Key infrastructure used included the Ethereum blockchain and various decentralised exchanges (DEXs).

The attack is attributed to the Lazarus Group, a well-known cybercrime syndicate with a history of targeting cryptocurrency platforms. This attribution is supported by the use of similar tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) observed in previous incidents linked to the group.
