On 26 August 2025, BetterBank, a prominent financial entity operating on multiple blockchain networks, experienced a significant cyber-attack resulting in the theft of approximately $5 million USD. The attack was executed by the hacker group APT38, known for its sophisticated cyber operations. The immediate financial impact was substantial, affecting both the institution and its clients.

The attack exploited a vulnerability within BetterBank's smart contract infrastructure, potentially involving a reentrancy attack or a flash loan exploit. The exact mechanism remains under investigation, but initial analysis suggests a failure in access control or private key compromise.

Stolen funds were rapidly moved through a series of transactions involving multiple blockchain networks and laundering stages. The attacker utilised known laundering techniques such as bridge hopping and mixer usage, eventually attempting to cash out through centralised exchanges.

APT38 is suspected to be behind the attack, with a high confidence level due to the use of sophisticated laundering techniques and infrastructure overlaps with previous incidents attributed to this group.
