On 22 March 2026, a significant crypto hack incident targeted the SolvBTC Exchange, resulting in the theft of approximately $25 million in various cryptocurrencies. The attack was executed by the hacker group APT38, known for its sophisticated cyber operations. The immediate financial impact was substantial, affecting both the exchange's operations and its users.

The attack exploited a vulnerability within the exchange's smart contract infrastructure, potentially involving a reentrancy attack or a flash loan exploit. The exact mechanism remains under investigation, but initial analysis suggests a failure in access control or private key compromise.

Stolen funds were rapidly moved through a series of transactions involving multiple blockchains and laundering techniques. The attacker utilised bridge hopping and mixer services to obfuscate the fund trail, eventually attempting to cash out through centralised exchanges.

APT38 is suspected with high confidence due to the use of known TTPs and infrastructure overlaps with previous incidents attributed to this group. The group's history of targeting financial institutions and their sophisticated laundering techniques support this attribution.
