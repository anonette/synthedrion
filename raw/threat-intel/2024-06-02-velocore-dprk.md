The Velocore incident involved a sophisticated crypto hack targeting a blockchain protocol, resulting in the theft of approximately $6.8 million USD. The attack occurred on 2 June 2024, and was attributed to the Lazarus Group, a well-known cybercriminal organisation. The immediate financial impact was significant, affecting both the protocol's operations and its users.

The attack exploited a vulnerability within the protocol's smart contract infrastructure, potentially involving access control failures or private key compromises. The exact exploit mechanism remains under investigation, but the rapid execution suggests premeditated planning and technical proficiency.

Stolen funds were quickly moved through a series of transactions involving multiple wallets and blockchain networks. The funds were layered using various techniques, including bridge hopping and mixer usage, before reaching centralised exchanges for cash-out. Key infrastructure used included the Poly Network and SushiSwap, among others.

The Lazarus Group is suspected with high confidence due to the use of known TTPs and infrastructure overlaps with previous incidents attributed to this group. The group's history of targeting financial institutions and blockchain protocols supports this attribution.
