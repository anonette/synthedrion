# M2 Exchange — DPRK-attributed incident

> Type: threat-intel incident (SOURCED CLAIM — treat attribution as a claim at the stated confidence, not fact)
> Incident ID: CHA-M2-Exchange-105
> Timestamp: 2024-10-31T00:00:00Z
> Attribution: DPRK / APT38 (confidence: 0.12)
> Target: M2 Exchange
> Amount (USD): $13,700,000
> Asset: Ethereum
> Vector: unknown
> References: M2 Exchange – Blockchain Forensics Intelligence Report (https://sandbox.hacksleuths.com/LLM_REPORTS/M2_Exchange/M2_Exchange_report.html)

On 31 October 2024, M2 Exchange experienced a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $13.7 million USD. The attack targeted the exchange's infrastructure, exploiting vulnerabilities to siphon funds from user accounts. The immediate financial impact was substantial, affecting both the exchange's liquidity and user confidence.

The attack was executed through a sophisticated exploitation of the exchange's security protocols. While the exact vulnerability exploited remains under investigation, the attack involved rapid transaction sequences and potential private key compromises. The use of automated scripts to facilitate the breach suggests a high level of technical proficiency.

Stolen funds were initially moved from the exploit wallet to intermediary wallets, employing complex layering techniques including the use of mixers and cross-chain bridges. The funds were subsequently distributed across multiple exchanges and laundering services to obfuscate their origin.

The attack is attributed to the hacker group APT38 with a medium confidence level. This attribution is based on the use of similar tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) observed in previous incidents linked to this group.
