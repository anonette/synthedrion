> References: DAO Maker – Blockchain Forensics Intelligence Report (https://sandbox.hacksleuths.com/LLM_REPORTS/DAO_Maker/DAO_Maker_report.html)

On 4 September 2021, DAO Maker, a prominent blockchain protocol, suffered a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $4,000,000.00 USD. The attack targeted the protocol's smart contract infrastructure, exploiting a vulnerability that allowed unauthorised access to user funds. The immediate financial impact was substantial, affecting both the protocol's operations and its user base.

The attack was executed through a sophisticated exploitation of DAO Maker's smart contract vulnerabilities. The attacker utilised a series of normal transactions to siphon funds from the protocol. The specific weakness exploited involved improper access controls, allowing the attacker to manipulate transaction flows without detection.

Stolen funds were rapidly moved through a series of transactions involving multiple wallets. The attacker employed a combination of layering techniques, including the use of intermediary wallets and potential mixing services, to obfuscate the fund trail. The funds were eventually directed towards exchanges and other cash-out points, indicating a premeditated laundering strategy.

The attack is suspected to be linked to the hacker group APT38, known for its sophisticated cyber operations and previous involvement in similar incidents. The use of advanced laundering techniques and infrastructure overlaps with known APT38 operations support this attribution.
