# Indexed Finance — DPRK-attributed incident

> Incident ID: CHA-Indexed-Finance-84
> Timestamp: 2021-10-14T00:00:00Z
> Attribution: DPRK / APT38 (confidence: 0.12)
> Target: Indexed Finance
> Amount (USD): $16,000,000
> Asset: Ethereum
> Vector: unknown
> References: Indexed Finance – Blockchain Forensics Intelligence Report (https://sandbox.hacksleuths.com/LLM_REPORTS/Indexed_Finance/Indexed_Finance_report.html)

On 14 October 2021, Indexed Finance, a decentralised finance (DeFi) protocol, suffered a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $16 million USD. The attack targeted the protocol's smart contracts, exploiting vulnerabilities to siphon funds from liquidity pools. The immediate financial impact was severe, affecting both the protocol's operations and its user base.

The attack was executed by exploiting a vulnerability in the smart contract logic of Indexed Finance. The attacker utilised a combination of flash loans and price manipulation techniques to artificially inflate asset values within the protocol, allowing them to withdraw more funds than they deposited. This exploit was facilitated by a lack of adequate safeguards against such manipulative tactics.

Following the exploit, the stolen funds were rapidly moved through a series of transactions involving multiple wallets and blockchain networks. The attacker employed sophisticated laundering techniques, including the use of decentralised exchanges (DEXs), cross-chain bridges, and mixing services to obfuscate the fund trail. The funds were eventually dispersed across various wallets, some of which were linked to known laundering infrastructure.

The attack is suspected to be linked to the hacker group APT38, known for its advanced persistent threat capabilities and previous involvement in similar DeFi exploits. The use of specific laundering techniques and infrastructure overlaps with past incidents attributed to this group supports this hypothesis.
