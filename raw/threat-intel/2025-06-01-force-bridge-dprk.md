The Force Bridge incident involved a sophisticated crypto hack targeting a blockchain bridge protocol, resulting in the theft of approximately $3,760,000.00 in digital assets. The attack was executed on 1 June 2025, with the Lazarus Group suspected of orchestrating the breach. The immediate financial impact was significant, affecting both the protocol's operations and its users.

The attack exploited a vulnerability in the Force Bridge protocol, potentially involving a smart contract flaw or private key compromise. The exact exploit mechanism remains under investigation, but the rapid execution suggests premeditated planning and technical expertise.

Stolen funds were quickly moved through a series of transactions involving multiple wallets, with laundering stages including the use of mixers and cross-chain bridges. The funds were eventually directed towards centralised exchanges for cash-out.

The Lazarus Group, a North Korean state-sponsored hacking group, is the primary suspect. This attribution is based on the group's known tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs), as well as infrastructure overlaps with previous incidents.
