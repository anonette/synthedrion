> References: Credix – Blockchain Forensics Intelligence Report (https://sandbox.hacksleuths.com/LLM_REPORTS/Credix/Credix_report.html)

On 4 August 2025, the Credix platform experienced a significant crypto hack resulting in the theft of approximately $4,500,000.00 USD. The attack targeted the platform's infrastructure, exploiting vulnerabilities to siphon funds into wallets controlled by the attackers. The immediate financial impact was substantial, affecting both the platform's liquidity and its users' assets.

The attack was executed through a series of sophisticated transactions that exploited weaknesses in the platform's security protocols. The exact mechanism involved the use of compromised private keys and potentially a reentrancy attack, allowing the attackers to drain funds without triggering immediate alarms. The use of advanced scripts and automated tools was evident in the rapid execution of transactions.

Stolen funds were initially moved through a series of intermediary wallets, employing techniques such as bridge hopping and mixer usage to obfuscate the trail. The funds traversed multiple blockchain networks, including Ethereum and Binance Smart Chain, before reaching centralised exchanges for cash-out.

The Lazarus Group, a well-known cybercrime syndicate, is suspected of orchestrating the attack. This attribution is based on the use of similar tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) observed in previous incidents attributed to the group, including the use of specific laundering methods and infrastructure overlaps.
