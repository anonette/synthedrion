On 31 July 2023, a significant crypto hack incident, referred to as "BALD on Base," was executed, targeting a blockchain protocol operating on the Base network. The attack resulted in the theft of approximately $23,000,000.00 USD. The exploit mechanism involved a sophisticated attack vector, potentially leveraging vulnerabilities within the protocol's smart contract infrastructure.

The attack was executed through a series of transactions that exploited a vulnerability in the protocol's smart contract. The specific weakness exploited remains under investigation, but initial analysis suggests potential manipulation of contract functions or access control failures. The attack was characterised by rapid transaction execution, indicating premeditated planning and execution.

Stolen funds were initially moved from the exploit wallet (0xccfa0530b9d52f970d1a2daea670ce58e4176389) through a series of intermediary wallets, employing techniques such as bridge hopping and mixer usage to obscure the trail. The funds were eventually directed towards centralised exchanges and other cash-out points.

The threat actor is suspected to be APT38, a group known for its sophisticated cyber capabilities and previous involvement in similar incidents. The attribution is supported by the use of advanced laundering techniques and infrastructure overlaps with past APT38 operations.
