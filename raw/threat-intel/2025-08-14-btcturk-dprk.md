On 14 August 2025, BTCTurk, a prominent cryptocurrency exchange, experienced a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $51.7 million USD in digital assets. The attack was executed by the Lazarus Group, a well-known cybercriminal organisation. The breach involved the exploitation of vulnerabilities within the exchange's infrastructure, leading to the unauthorised transfer of funds to multiple external wallets.

The attack was executed through a sophisticated exploitation of BTCTurk's security systems, potentially involving compromised private keys or vulnerabilities in smart contract implementations. The exact technical details of the exploit remain under investigation, but the rapid execution suggests premeditated planning and advanced technical capabilities.

Following the initial theft, the stolen funds were rapidly moved through a series of transactions involving multiple intermediary wallets. The funds were laundered using a combination of mixers, cross-chain bridges, and exchanges, including the use of known laundering infrastructure. The funds were eventually dispersed across various exchanges, complicating recovery efforts.

The Lazarus Group is suspected of orchestrating the attack, with a high level of confidence based on the use of known tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) associated with the group. The group's history of targeting cryptocurrency exchanges and the specific laundering patterns observed align with previous incidents attributed to them.
