On 8 January 2022, a significant crypto hack targeted the LCX platform, resulting in the theft of approximately $7,940,000.00 USD. The attack involved the exploitation of vulnerabilities within the platform's infrastructure, leading to the unauthorised transfer of funds from LCX's wallets to addresses controlled by the attackers.

The attack was executed through a series of transactions that exploited a vulnerability in the LCX platform's smart contract or wallet management system. The specific exploit mechanism remains under investigation, but it likely involved either a smart contract flaw or a compromised private key, allowing the attackers to initiate unauthorised transfers.

Stolen funds were rapidly moved through a series of transactions involving multiple intermediary wallets. The attackers utilised known laundering techniques such as mixer services and cross-chain bridges to obfuscate the fund flow. The funds were eventually directed towards exchanges and other cash-out points, making recovery efforts challenging.

While the identity of the attackers remains unknown, the use of sophisticated laundering techniques suggests involvement by a well-organised group with prior experience in crypto-related crimes. The presence of high-confidence matches in offshore leaks databases indicates potential connections to known money laundering networks.
