The Infini incident involved a significant crypto hack targeting a blockchain protocol on 24 February 2024. The attack resulted in the theft of approximately $49.5 million USD, impacting the protocol's operations and its user base. The exploit mechanism remains unknown, but the financial impact was immediate and substantial, affecting the protocol's liquidity and user trust.

The attack was executed through a series of transactions that exploited a vulnerability within the protocol's infrastructure. The specific weakness exploited is not detailed in the available data, but the attack involved rapid fund transfers and the use of sophisticated laundering techniques.

Stolen funds were moved from the exploit wallet through a series of laundering stages, involving multiple blockchain networks, bridges, and potentially mixers. The funds were eventually directed towards centralised exchanges for cash-out. Key infrastructure used includes bridge contracts and high-volume intermediary addresses.

The attack is suspected to be linked to the hacker group APT38, known for similar tactics and infrastructure usage. The attribution is supported by transaction patterns and the use of known laundering techniques. Confidence in this attribution is medium, pending further verification.
