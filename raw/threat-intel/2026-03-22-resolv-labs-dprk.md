On 22 March 2026, Resolv Labs, a blockchain protocol, experienced a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $25 million USD in various cryptocurrencies. The attack was executed by the hacker group APT38, known for its sophisticated cyber operations. The immediate financial impact was severe, affecting the protocol's liquidity and user trust.

The attack exploited a vulnerability in Resolv Labs' smart contract infrastructure, potentially involving a reentrancy attack or a flash loan exploit. The exact mechanism remains under investigation, but initial analysis suggests a failure in access control or improper handling of contract functions.

Stolen funds were rapidly moved through a series of transactions involving multiple blockchains and bridges, including Ethereum and Binance Smart Chain. The attacker utilised known laundering techniques such as bridge hopping and mixer usage to obscure the fund trail.

APT38 is suspected with high confidence due to the use of sophisticated laundering techniques and infrastructure overlaps with previous incidents attributed to this group. Their known tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) align with the observed attack patterns.
