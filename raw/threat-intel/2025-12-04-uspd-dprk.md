The USPD incident involved a significant crypto hack targeting a blockchain protocol, resulting in the theft of substantial funds. The attack occurred on 4 December 2025, with the immediate financial impact involving the loss of approximately $1,000,000.00 per transaction, as evidenced by multiple transactions of this amount. The exploit mechanism remains unknown, but the involvement of the hacker group APT38 is suspected.

The attack was executed through a series of transactions originating from a primary wallet address, 0x083379bdac3e138cb0c7210e0282fbc466a3215a, which acted as a critical bridge in the operation. The technical details of the vulnerability exploited are not explicitly documented in the source material.

Stolen funds were moved rapidly through a network of intermediary wallets, employing techniques such as bridge hopping and layering to obscure the trail. The funds traversed multiple chains and utilised various bridges, including known laundering infrastructures. The final destinations of these funds remain under investigation, with some reaching centralised exchanges.

APT38, a known cybercriminal group, is suspected of orchestrating this attack. This attribution is based on the group's historical tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs), which align with the observed transaction patterns and infrastructure usage. The confidence level in this attribution is medium due to the lack of direct evidence linking the group to the specific transactions.
