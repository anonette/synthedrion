On 12 September 2023, CoinEx, a cryptocurrency exchange, experienced a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $54.3 million in digital assets. The attack was executed by exploiting vulnerabilities within the exchange's infrastructure, leading to the unauthorised transfer of funds to wallets controlled by the attackers. The immediate financial impact was substantial, affecting both the exchange's operations and its user base.

The attack was executed through a sophisticated exploitation of CoinEx's security protocols. The attackers utilised a combination of private key compromise and infrastructure weaknesses to gain access to the exchange's wallets. The specific vulnerability exploited remains under investigation, but initial analysis suggests a potential failure in access control mechanisms.

Following the exploit, the stolen funds were rapidly moved through a series of transactions involving multiple wallets and blockchain networks. The attackers employed various laundering techniques, including bridge hopping and mixer usage, to obfuscate the fund flow. Key infrastructure used included decentralised exchanges (DEXs) and cross-chain bridges, facilitating the movement of assets across different blockchains.

The attack is attributed to the hacker group APT38, known for its sophisticated cyber operations and previous involvement in similar incidents. The attribution is supported by the use of known TTPs and infrastructure overlaps with past attacks linked to the group. The confidence level in this attribution is high, given the consistency of the attack patterns with APT38's modus operandi.
