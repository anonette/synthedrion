On 15 July 2025, a significant crypto hack incident targeted the BigONE platform, resulting in the theft of approximately $27 million USD. The attack was executed by exploiting vulnerabilities within the platform's infrastructure, leading to unauthorised fund transfers. The immediate financial impact was substantial, affecting both the platform's operations and its users.

The attack was executed through a series of coordinated transactions that exploited a vulnerability in the platform's smart contract infrastructure. The specific weakness involved was likely related to access control failures, allowing the attacker to initiate unauthorised transactions. The use of sophisticated scripts and tools was evident, indicating a high level of technical expertise.

Stolen funds were initially moved from the exploit wallet to various intermediary wallets, employing a strategy of rapid multi-hop transactions. The funds were subsequently laundered through a combination of mixers and cross-chain bridges, eventually reaching centralised exchanges for cash-out. Key infrastructure used included known laundering services and high-volume intermediaries.

The Lazarus Group, a well-known cybercrime organisation, is suspected of orchestrating the attack. This attribution is based on the use of similar tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) observed in previous incidents attributed to the group. The group's known infrastructure and operational patterns align with those observed in this case.
