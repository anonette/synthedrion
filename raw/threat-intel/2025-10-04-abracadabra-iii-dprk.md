On 25 March 2025, a significant blockchain exploit occurred, targeting the Abracadabra protocol. The attack resulted in the theft of approximately $12,913,691.00. The exploit was executed by the hacker group APT38, known for their sophisticated cyber operations.

The attack exploited a vulnerability within the Abracadabra protocol, although the specific technical details of the vulnerability remain unknown. The transactions were executed rapidly, indicating a pre-planned and automated attack strategy.

Stolen funds were initially moved through a series of rapid transactions across multiple wallets, primarily using the Ethereum blockchain. The funds were then layered through various intermediary wallets and potentially laundered using decentralised exchanges and mixers.

APT38 is suspected to be behind the attack, with a high confidence level due to the use of known TTPs associated with this group, including rapid transaction sequences and the use of sophisticated laundering techniques.
