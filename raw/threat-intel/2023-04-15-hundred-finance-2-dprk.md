On 15 April 2023, Hundred Finance, a decentralised finance (DeFi) protocol, experienced a significant security breach resulting in the theft of approximately $7.4 million USD. The attack was executed by exploiting a vulnerability within the protocol's smart contract infrastructure, leading to the unauthorised transfer of funds to an attacker-controlled wallet.

The attack leveraged a smart contract vulnerability, potentially involving reentrancy or flash loan exploits, although specific technical details of the exploit mechanism were not provided in the source data. The attacker utilised a series of rapid transactions to drain funds from the protocol, indicating a high level of technical sophistication and pre-planning.

Following the exploit, the stolen funds were quickly moved through a series of intermediary wallets and potentially laundered using decentralised exchanges (DEXs) and cross-chain bridges. The funds were dispersed across multiple blockchain networks to obfuscate the trail and complicate recovery efforts.

The attack is attributed to the Lazarus Group, a well-known cybercrime syndicate with a history of targeting cryptocurrency platforms. This attribution is based on transaction patterns and wallet behaviours consistent with previous incidents linked to the group.
