The Raft incident involved a significant crypto hack targeting a blockchain protocol, resulting in the theft of approximately $3,300,000.00 USD. The attack was executed on 10 November 2023, affecting a major DeFi platform known for its substantial Total Value Locked (TVL) and extensive user base. The immediate financial impact was severe, with funds rapidly moved through various laundering stages.

The attack exploited a vulnerability in the protocol's smart contract, potentially involving a reentrancy attack or access control failure. The attacker utilised automated scripts to execute transactions swiftly, indicating a high level of technical sophistication and premeditation.

Stolen funds were initially moved from the exploit wallet to intermediary wallets, employing a series of rapid transactions across multiple blockchains. The attacker utilised known laundering techniques such as bridge hopping and mixer usage to obfuscate the fund trail. The funds eventually reached centralised exchanges (CEXs) for cash-out.

The threat actor remains unidentified, but the attack's sophistication suggests involvement by a well-resourced group with prior experience in similar exploits. The use of advanced laundering techniques and infrastructure overlaps with known threat actors increase the confidence in this assessment.
