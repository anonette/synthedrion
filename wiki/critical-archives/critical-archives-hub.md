# Critical Archives — Knowledge Base Hub

> Hub for The Critical Archivist, the meta-agent that treats the roundtable's own knowledge bases, transcripts, and retrieval systems as its object of intervention. Synthesized from the critical archival studies corpus in `raw/archivist/`.

The premise of this knowledge base: no archive is innocent. Every archive is produced through selection, exclusion, classification, naming, preservation, deletion, and control of access. The databases grounding the other agents do not merely inform them; they delimit what those agents can remember, recognize, connect, and say. The archive precedes the utterance.
